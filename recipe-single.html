Fluffy Japanese Soufflé Pancakes

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 20 min
Serves: 2
4.8 (124 reviews)
Prep Time
15 min
Cook Time
20 min
Servings
2
Difficulty
Easy

These ultra-fluffy, cloud-like Japanese soufflé pancakes are the Instagram-worthy breakfast you've been dreaming about. Jiggly, pillowy, and delicately sweet — they're worlds apart from your regular pancakes. The secret is a perfectly stiff meringue folded gently into a smooth batter, then cooked low and slow with a lid on to create that legendary height.

Ingredients

2 large eggs, separated
2 tbsp whole milk
½ tsp vanilla extract
¼ cup (30g) all-purpose flour
½ tsp baking powder
3 tbsp (36g) caster sugar
¼ tsp cream of tartar
1 tbsp neutral oil (for the pan)
Icing sugar, whipped cream & fresh berries, to serve

Instructions

1

Prepare the yolk mixture

In a medium bowl, whisk the egg yolks, milk, and vanilla extract together until smooth. Sift in the flour and baking powder, then mix gently until just combined. Set aside.

2

Make the meringue

In a clean, grease-free bowl, beat the egg whites with the cream of tartar using a hand mixer on medium speed. Gradually add the caster sugar and increase to high speed, beating until stiff, glossy peaks form — about 3–4 minutes.

3

Fold together gently

Add one third of the meringue to the yolk mixture and stir to loosen. Gently fold in the remaining meringue in two additions using a rubber spatula, keeping as much air in the batter as possible. Stop folding when just combined — streaks are fine.

4

Cook low and slow

Heat a non-stick pan over the lowest heat setting and brush lightly with oil. Spoon the batter into two tall mounds (use a ring mould if available). Cover with a lid and cook for 5–6 minutes until the base is set and golden. Carefully flip with a wide spatula, cover again, and cook for another 4–5 minutes.

5

Serve immediately

Transfer the pancakes to a plate and dust generously with icing sugar. Serve straight away with a dollop of whipped cream and fresh berries — they deflate quickly, so don't wait!
